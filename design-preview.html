System laws

the rules that keep it coherent — including what was tried and rejected

Teal is scarce. Live, active, focus, provenance, identity — never decoration, never a functional state.

Green/amber/red carry meaning. Pass, warn, breach. They never mix with teal and never mark liveness.

Every number is mono + tabular-nums, labeled by an uppercase micro-label. 10px is the type floor.

One motion curve — cubic-bezier(.22, 1, .36, 1). Announce, then rest: decision surfaces go still.

Dashed hairline = sandbox. The what-if panel and term amendments are scratchpads, not the record.

Dark-mode law: alpha borders vanish on graphite — frame tinted elements with the solid color; ghost buttons on tint get a solid panel fill.

Rejected, do not resurrect: a serif document voice, dimming content behind the gate, glass surfaces, theme-reveal transitions, decorative progress rules.

One typeface family. Inter for the voice, Geist Mono for the record. Nothing else.
